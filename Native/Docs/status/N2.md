## Contract and file requests (for N0 / the integrator)

1. `ScrollsService/TaskRegistry.swift`: add `AdapterLoadTask.self, DemoLoadTask.self, BootstrapCipherTask.self`
   (ScrollsService/Pipelines). Without them the live app answers E_JOB_TYPE for `demo-load`, `adapter-load` and
   `bootstrap-cipher` (the CLI and the self-tests run them through `AtlasEngine`, whose registry adds them).
2. `ScrollsService/LiveAtlas.swift` `open`: after the atlas opens, when `launch.startBootstrap` (false in self-tests
   and UI tests), call `_ = await BootstrapCipher.start(live)` — it returns at once and queues phases A → A2 → B when the
   bundled `cipher-meta` pack is present (health.cipher then reports the phase); in `close()`, `BootstrapCipher.stop(self)`.
   This is what makes "phase A finishes and Switch to Cipher enables" happen in the live app (N8's first-minute step reads
   health.cipher).
3. `DIVERGENCES.json`: register N2-1 — `original_file.rel_path` and `.source_path` are `pack:<name>@<sha8>/<member>` and
   the member path on the phone vs `originals/<aa>/<sha>` and a Mac path in the reference (IOS-SPEC §3.5, by design);
   N2-2 — the number of `import_batch` rows of a chunked load and their `options_json`/`summary_json` depend on the
   machine's speed (≈ 20 s chunks on both sides); the slice comparison collapses batches by natural key. Both rules are
   applied in `CipherSliceTests` only.
4. `dump-spec.json`: rules for raw ids inside text (`label_tri.ref` 'document:12', `created_by` 'run:7', …) — until then
   `CipherSliceTests` maps both sides' ids through the id-free keys of `slices.mjs` `ID_MAP_SQL`.
